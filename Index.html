📘 Slab reference & assumptions

Current law: Rs. 1,800,000 personal relief, then 6% on the first Rs. 1,000,000, 18%, 24% and 30% on each next Rs. 500,000, and 36% above that. Qualifying foreign-currency income remitted through a Sri Lankan bank is capped at 15%. The Budget 2027 option is an illustrative preview (Rs. 1,950,000 relief, 12% on the second band) and not enacted law.

Rental uses a 25% allowance or actual repairs. Credits entered are taken as given, and a refund is shown when credits exceed tax. This tool gives estimates only, not tax advice. Please confirm figures with the IRD or a tax advisor. Return for 2025/26 is due 30 November 2026, with any balance payable by 30 September 2026. Rent AIT (10%) and the 5% fee WHT apply above Rs. 100,000 a month. Government donations are deducted in full and charity donations are limited to the lower of Rs. 75,000 or one-third of taxable income (estimated here after relief). Capital gains tax (15% from 3 June 2026) is filed separately and is not included. Employee EPF, medical, school fees, insurance and home-loan interest are not deductible.
